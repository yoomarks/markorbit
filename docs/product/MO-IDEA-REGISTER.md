For every new material idea add:

- ID;
- idea;
- user/problem;
- expected value;
- current evidence;
- dependencies;
- Product Director view;
- Design Director view;
- Technical Director view;
- disposition: PROMOTED / PARTIALLY_PROMOTED / MR-1.0 / MR-1.5 / WS-1.0 / WS-2.0 / WS-3.0 / RESEARCH / PARKED / REJECTED;
- date and decision-log link when resolved.
