## Products

### MarkOrbit Lite

Professional product for trademark practitioners. Beta pillars:

- Today;
- Content Studio;
- Opportunity Center;
- Trademark Assets;
- Capability Center;
- AI Guide;
- professional MarkReg intake and work management.

### markreg.com

Direct-customer product for international trademark filing and lifecycle management. Beta journey:

```text
Anonymous Consultation
→ Account / Draft Recovery
→ Guided Intake
→ A/B/C Recommendation
→ Quote and Confirmation
→ Documents and Customer Actions
→ Order / Matter
→ Status, Evidence and Lifecycle
```

## Shared backend, separate relationship model

Both products consume the MarkReg service, but they must preserve separate:

- Channel;
- Relationship Owner;
- Contracting Party;
- Payment Receiver;
- Delivery Owner;
- Communication Owner;
- Customer-facing Brand;
- Professional Authority.

## Beta loops

1. Content loop;
2. Opportunity loop;
3. Direct-customer application loop;
4. Professional application loop;
5. Capability learning loop.

## Explicit non-goals

- automatic official filing;
- payment custody or escrow;
- public provider marketplace or star ranking;
- automatic professional qualification;
- automatic Capability verification or canon mutation;
- complete global jurisdiction coverage;
- production GA claim.
